🏙️ Intellicity - Smart Urban Management System
Bridging the gap between citizens and city administration for a smarter, more transparent future.

📖 Overview
Intellicity is a dual-platform urban management ecosystem designed to streamline communication between city residents and administrative officials. By digitizing the grievance redressal process, Intellicity tackles long-standing civic challenges such as administrative delays and lack of transparency.

The system consists of two synchronized components:

1) Android Mobile Application: Empowers citizens to report community issues with multimedia evidence.
2) Web Administration Portal: Enables officials to view, manage, and resolve grievances in real-time.

✨ Key Features
📱 Citizen Mobile App (Android)
1. Multimedia Reporting: Citizens can capture and upload photos of civic issues (e.g., potholes, waste accumulation) directly through the app.
2. Real-time Tracking: Users can track the status of their submitted grievances (Submitted → In Progress → Resolved) in real-time.
3. Department Categorization: Automatic sorting of issues into categories like Electricity, Water Supply, and Waste Management.
4. Broadcasts: Receive official announcements and updates directly from the city administration.

💻 Admin Web Portal
1. Centralized Dashboard: A "Bird's Eye View" of all city operations with statistical breakdowns of pending vs. resolved cases.
2. Grievance Management: Admins can update the status of complaints (e.g., mark as "In Progress" or "Resolved") and view attached evidence.
3. Digital Announcements: Officials can publish city-wide broadcasts that instantly appear on citizens' mobile devices.
4. Secure Access: Role-based authentication for department officials.
